Privacy by product design

Privacy is more than a country on a footer.

Abrolia handles messages about your family. Trust has to be visible in what enters, what leaves, and who gets to decide.

i

We will not call Abrolia zero-knowledge: to explain a message, the service has to read it. We would rather be precise than theatrical.

01

You approve every external action

Nothing is sent or created outside Abrolia without confirmation from an authorised adult.

02

One isolated space per household

The pilot architecture separates each family’s application, storage and credentials.

03

No model training on family content

Abrolia is designed around commercial model APIs whose terms exclude customer data from model training.

04

Honest processor disclosure

The core service is designed for EU hosting; international specialist providers will be named rather than hidden behind an “EU-only” claim.

Pilot / 2026

A careful pilot, not a rushed launch

Real family messages come after the safeguards.

Abrolia currently runs on synthetic test data. Real correspondence will be connected only after legal review, processor agreements and international-transfer safeguards are complete.
